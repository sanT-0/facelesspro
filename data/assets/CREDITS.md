## License Definitions

- **CC0 (Creative Commons Zero)**: Public domain dedication. No attribution required, but credited here as best practice.
- **CC-BY-3.0 / CC-BY-4.0 (Creative Commons Attribution)**: Requires attribution to the creator.
- **SIL OFL 1.1 (Open Font License)**: Permits free use, modification, and distribution of fonts.

## Asset Storage

Assets are referenced by URL/path in the API. Actual files are not bundled in the repository due to size. They should be downloaded and placed in the appropriate directories:

- Background videos: `DATA_DIR/backgrounds/`
- Sound effects: `DATA_DIR/audio/sfx/`
- Fonts: `packages/art/fonts/ttf/`

## Verification

Run `npm run check:assets` (to be implemented) to verify all referenced assets exist and have valid licenses.
